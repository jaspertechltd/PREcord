🎨 Palette: Use Modifier.toggleable for better accessibility

💡 What: Replaced `Modifier.clickable(role = Role.Switch)` with `Modifier.toggleable` in `SettingsToggleRow` within `SettingsSheet.kt`.
🎯 Why: `Modifier.clickable` does not fully support screen reader announcements for checked/unchecked states on toggleable components, whereas `Modifier.toggleable` correctly updates state for accessibility services.
📸 Before/After: Visuals are unchanged. The improvement is primarily structural and semantic for screen readers.
♿ Accessibility: Ensures that screen readers accurately announce whether the toggle row is checked or unchecked by leveraging the native `Modifier.toggleable`.
